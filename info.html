Requirements

To earn your cup of boba, your completed website will need to follow certain technical requirements~
According to Hack Club:

  • You gotta have at least 5 HTML & CSS features, and one of them has to be CSS.
  • You can't use AI to write your website.
  • It has to be good effort and "accessible" - for example, you can't have too many small fonts, color changes, etc.

Submission

When all is said and done, you are satisfied with your website and have published it as on Git Pages (refer to Tech support page for help), Submit 4 Boba Here!

***You will not be able to obtain your free boba immediately, as it takes some time for Hack Club to review submissions!!
